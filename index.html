biography

cam palermo is an independent songwriter and recording artist developing a body of work rooted in intimate vocals, understated arrangements and close attention to everyday detail. The songs pair a direct lyrical approach with an atmospheric, personal sound.

The current demos introduce a project centred on connection, uncertainty and the small shifts that shape a life. A forthcoming EP, scheduled for 2027, will bring these ideas together in a first cohesive release.
